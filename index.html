FAQ

Questions, answered.

How much does it cost?

A one-time setup fee plus $297/month for hosting and your AI lead engine (website, AI chat and instant callback). The 24/7 AI Receptionist is an optional add-on. Your setup fee depends on your company's size and needs, and you'll get an exact number on your call.

Do I really see my website before paying?

Yes. We build a free preview of your new homepage and walk you through it on our call. If you move forward, you sign your proposal and pay the setup fee, and we build out the full site. If not, you owe nothing.

How fast can I go live?

Most sites launch within 3 days of your setup payment and onboarding. You send photos and details; we handle the writing, design and setup.

Do you work with contractors in my area?

Yes. We work with contractors across the U.S. Everything is done remotely, and every site is built around your service areas and local market.

Does the AI receptionist work with my current number?

Yes. Calls you don't answer can forward to your AI receptionist, which answers, collects the details, books the appointment and texts you a summary.

Am I locked into a contract?

No long-term contracts. You can cancel anytime after month 3, and your website is backed by our money-back guarantee.
