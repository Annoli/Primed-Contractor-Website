Primed Contractor Agency ("Primed," "we," "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what we collect through primedcontractor.com and how we use it.

Information we collect

Information you give us, such as your name, company, phone number, email, trade and website when you fill out a form or chat with us, plus basic technical data (browser type, pages visited) collected automatically.

How we use it

To respond to your request, book calls, build your website preview, provide our services, send service-related messages, and improve our website.

Calls and text messages

If you give consent, we may contact you by phone and SMS, including automated messages, about your request. Message frequency varies. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out or HELP for help. Consent is not a condition of purchase.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties.

Sharing

We share information only with service providers that help us run our business (for example, our CRM, hosting and communication tools), under confidentiality obligations, or when required by law. We do not sell your personal information.

Security & retention

We use reasonable safeguards to protect your information and keep it only as long as needed for the purposes above.

Your choices

You can ask us to access, correct or delete your information, or stop contacting you, by emailing hello@primedcontractor.com.
